How this works

Each page's design and layout is hardcoded in its own template file (e.g. templates/home.html). The only thing admin can change is the text and images inside each box - open a page, turn on edit mode, click into any text to edit it or change its image, then press "Save changes" on that box.

Go to Pages and content to add, edit, hide, reorder or delete pages. Change the site name, colors, contact details and footer under Site settings.
